> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Being Git-backed changes nothing for your readers**
>
> They get a knowledge base site, search, categories and the ability to tell you a page was unhelpful
> — the same reader-facing product as the editor-first product type. The repository is where your
> content lives, not something readers experience.

## The one habit worth building

Read what readers search for. It is the only source of information about your documentation that
comes from outside your own assumptions, and it is usually a list of pages you have not written.

> [!TIP]
> **Searches that return nothing are your backlog, in the reader's own words**
>
> They arrive ranked by how many people wanted them, with their titles already written. Nothing else
> you can look at tells you so directly what to do next.
